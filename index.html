Before You Play

Player Primer

What you need to know before making a character.

The Basics

D&D 5th Edition, 2024 rules. Sheets on D&D Beyond. Dark fantasy with a cheerful surface, mystery and magic with something a little unsettling underneath.

The World

Elthizar Adventurer Academy — founded by Euduneus, deep roots in magic and monster fighting. Groups travel by dragon, solo/paired by pegasus. Curfew enforced, chimeras banned.

Other places: Kirael (fae kingdom), Vardok (dragonkin city), Evron (human settlement, tense with Vardok).

Tone and Content, Honestly

Loss and grief as backstory · political corruption and class injustice · psychological horror over gore. If this doesn't sound fun, session zero is the time to say so.

Safety Tools

Our safe word is COCONUT. Say it and we pause, change, or skip whatever's happening, no explanation needed. Messaging the DM after session works too.

Rumours & Mysteries

  • Something lurks in the forest near the academy.
  • The paintings below the Herbology classroom cry, for reasons no one's explained.
  • The river near the academy is making people sick.
  • There's a locked door in the headmaster's office, and nobody knows what opens it.

Friendship Points

Build real relationships with major NPCs through genuine roleplay. Reaching new milestones with them unlocks a deeper scene. Happens naturally through play.

Quick Reference

How to Play D&D

What it is

A collaborative story. The DM describes the world and plays every character who isn't yours. You decide what your character does. Dice decide uncertain outcomes.

The basic loop

DM describes the scene → you say what you do → roll if uncertain → DM narrates the result.

The dice

d20 + modifier vs. a DC (target number) set by the DM. Meet or beat it, you succeed.

Character sheet, in short

Six ability scores (Str/Dex/Con/Int/Wis/Cha) · skills tied to those abilities · HP (damage you can take) · AC (how hard you are to hit).

In combat

Roll initiative → on your turn take an action and usually a move → repeat until it ends.

Roleplay is just talking

Speak as your character, make choices as your character would, ask questions when curious.
